## Measurements

Numbers from the running app, not estimates. Ask for more and I will measure
rather than guess — that is how GAP-061 went wrong in 1.7.

### GAP-090, which files are missing (83 prints)

Flagging what is **missing** is quiet; flagging what is **present** is not:

| icons on a tile | missing | present |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 70% | — |
| 1 | 24% | 4% |
| 2 | 4% | 8% |
| 3 | 2% | 51% |
| 4 | — | 37% |

Per type: no G-code **16%**, no STL **5%**, no 3MF **4%**. Every combination
that occurs: G-code alone 16%, STL alone 5%, 3MF alone 4%, G-code+3MF 2%,
G-code+3MF+STL 2%, G-code+STL 1%. So 70% of tiles would carry nothing and
almost all the rest carry one icon.

Two parts of the original idea were dropped **because** of the measurement, both
reversing the first instinct. **Video** is out: it would have marked **40%** of
tiles, and the owner does not upload a timelapse for every print. **"Incomplete
3MF"** is out: a proxy for unsliced matched **92%**, because a downloaded
project file is normally unsliced and the G-code beside it carries the slice.

### GAP-071, the dark tint

| `--mix` | ground | darkest filament | lightest filament |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 100% (default) | `rgb(0,0,0)` | 1.21 | 1.57 |
| 80% | `rgb(22,24,30)` | **1.02** | 1.33 |
| 60% | `rgb(44,49,60)` | 1.34 | **1.03** |
| 40% (the 1.9 floor) | `rgb(67,73,90)` | 1.94 | 1.49 |
| 25% | `rgb(83,92,112)` | 2.60 | 2.00 |

No single mix clears 3:1 at both ends, and at 80% and 60% the result is worse
than doing nothing. The ground alone cannot carry this. It needs a direction —
an outline, a plate behind the model, a different encoding of filament colour —
not another number.

### GAP-098, a menu painted over

At 1360px the leftmost column menu in Toegang spans x=364 to x=504 while the
sticky user column ends at x=410, so **46px of the panel is covered** and the
items read "s selecteren". The scroll box does not clip it: the sticky cell
paints over it, although `z-popover` (30) sits well above `z-raised` (1). The
system needs to say how a popover escapes a sticky column.

### GAP-104, text in an uploaded SVG

The container ships **no fonts at all** (`/usr/share/fonts` absent, `fc-list`
returns 0) and the rasteriser gets no font configuration. An SVG holding one
rectangle and one word rendered **1600 opaque pixels for the shape and 0 for the
text**. Most logo files are a mark plus a wordmark, so the common case is a logo
that uploads "successfully" and comes back partly blank, on screen and on every
document. This has a product half (ship fonts, or refuse such a file) and a
design half (the refusal or warning state and its copy). I need the design half
whichever way the product half goes.

### GAP-105, what a logo must satisfy

One file is shown on `surface` in both themes with no adaptation, and on white
paper in every document. The obvious choices fail silently: a black wordmark
vanishes on the dark sidebar, a white one on the invoice. Your own `primary`
cannot do it either — `#3659c9` is 6.12:1 on white but **2.79:1** on `#1a1b26`;
`#7aa2f7` is 6.79:1 on the dark ground but **2.52:1** on white. A mid tone can:
`#5878d6` gives 4.14:1 and 4.13:1. Also, the sidebar caps the logo at
`--space-7`, so a tagline lands near 4px — I drew one and removed it after
seeing it at that size. Either state the contract, or solve it structurally.

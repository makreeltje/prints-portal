DatePicker picks a single date or the start and end of a period; the field shows `5 okt 2026` and also accepts `d-m-jjjj`. It is used for Van and Tot en met in the invoice export and for dates in filters.

**Anatomy.** **Field:** `pp-iwrap` with `pp-input nl has-r` and a calendar icon button on the right; the label sits above, the optional help or error below. **Calendar popover** (`pp-pop pp-calpop`, 296px, `surface`, `border`, `radius-lg`, `shadow-md`, in a `.pp-anchor` so it floats at `z-popover`): a month header `.pp-cal-hd` with the ghost icon buttons "Vorige maand" and "Volgende maand" around the month name (`text-strong`, 600); below it `.pp-cal`, a 7-column grid with a 4px gap that starts on Monday. The weekday row (`.h`: Ma Di Wo Do Vr Za Zo) is `text-xs` in `muted`; a day is a `button.d` (`text-sm`, tabular figures, `radius-md`, height `--pp-tap`: 36px, 44px at 480px and below). Hover is `primary-tint-soft`; today (`.t`) has a 2px inset ring in `primary`; the selected day (`.s`) is `primary` with `on-primary` text; days outside the allowed range are `disabled` (`muted`, `opacity-disabled`, not focusable). Blank cells before the 1st are empty `span`s. A footer row has the ghost buttons "Vandaag" and "Wissen". The popover opens below the field and flips above (`.up`) when there is no room; at 480px and below it is a bottom sheet (`z-overlay`). The period is checked: Tot en met cannot be before Van ("Kies een datum binnen de periode.").

**Tokens.** `surface`, `border`, `primary`, `on-primary`, `primary-tint-soft`, `muted`, `focus-ring`, `shadow-md`, `radius-lg`, `radius-md`, `space-1`, `space-2`, `space-3`, `text-sm`, `text-xs`, `opacity-disabled`, `z-popover`. Classes `pp-calpop`, `pp-cal-hd`, `pp-cal`, `pp-cal-ft`.

**States.** Day: default, hover, focus-visible, selected, today, today and selected, disabled (out of range). Field: default, focus, error, filled. Popover: closed, open below, open above, bottom sheet.

**Accessibility.** The grid has `role="grid"` with `columnheader` and `gridcell` children; the focused day has `tabindex="0"`, the others `-1`; arrow keys move by day and week, PageUp and PageDown by month, Home and End to the start and end of the week, Enter selects, Esc closes and returns focus to the input; today has `aria-current="date"`, the selected day `aria-selected="true"`; every day has a full `aria-label` ("5 oktober 2026"); typing `d-m-jjjj` in the field works without opening the calendar.

PrintCard is one print in the grid view: thumbnail, identity, key numbers, status and tags.

**Anatomy.** Thumbnail (4:3, server-rendered PNG tinted with the spool colour; see the tint rule under SpoolSwatch; or a "Geen voorbeeld" fallback), selection checkbox top-left on a `surface` chip, name (600), "klant / project" (`muted`), meta line (materiaal, print time with clock icon, grams), tags, then the price block on the left and the status badge on the right. Fluid width 220–320px; the grid uses `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(240px, 1fr))`, one column at 480px and below. **Deels betaald:** price block shows the remaining amount with the `pp-remaining` line, badge Deels betaald. **Niet factureren:** the price block shows "Kosten € x,xx" and the badge Niet factureren replaces the payment badge. **Verwijderd** (admin filter "Toon verwijderde"): thumbnail at 45% opacity, badge Verwijderd and a secondary small button "Herstellen" (one print; projects and customers have Herstellen in their header). **Gearchiveerd:** badge Gearchiveerd, thumbnail at 60% opacity. **Op factuur:** a locked print also shows the info badge "Op factuur {nummer}" in the tag row. **Mogelijk dubbel** appears only on the detail page.

**Tokens.** `surface`, `border`, `primary-tint-soft`/`primary` (selected), `primary-tint-strong` (live flash), `shadow-sm` (hover), `radius-lg`, `space-3`.

**States.** Default, hover, focus-visible (card link), selected, live update (1.6s flash, no layout shift), missing data, partial, niet factureren, archived, deleted.

**Accessibility.** The card is a link to the detail page with the name as accessible text; the checkbox and Herstellen are separate controls. Status is text plus icon.

**Orders (1.4).** A print with more than one order shows the **sum** of the order prices, the **least settled** status of its orders (Open before Deels betaald before Betaald; Afgeschreven counts as settled), the open amount over all orders ("Nog € x open") and "{n} bestellingen" in the meta line. A deleted Nabestelling no longer counts.

**1.9.** (2026-10-07) **Tint floor (GAP-071, proposed; needs your measurement).** The thumbnail's ground is the filament colour, which hides a model on a dark spool. Rule: the app computes the relative luminance **L** of the filament colour (WCAG formula) and sets `--mix` on the thumbnail: **L ≥ 0.18: `--mix: 100%`** (colour as is); below that **`--mix = max(40%, L / 0.18 × 100%)`**. The ground is then `color-mix(in srgb, var(--c1) var(--mix), var(--border-strong))`: a dark spool is blended towards `border-strong`, a mid blue-grey whose luminance is about 0.17 to 0.19 in **both** themes, so the floor is the same in light and dark and no new token is needed. The existing 28% white sheen stays, and a 1px inset `border-strong` ring at 50% opacity (`::after`) keeps a black tile separate from the card. A black spool is a grey ground (40% black), a dark purple is a dusky purple: still recognisable, no longer swallowing the model. Light filaments (L ≥ 0.18) are unchanged. `--mix` is a new allowed inline data property next to `--c1`. **To measure (owner):** the rendered model colour of a thumbnail (a dark grey model) and the ground colour of a black and of a dark purple tile, in the app after this change; I need the contrast ratio between them, aiming at 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11 for a graphic). If a dark model on the 40% floor is below 3:1, raise the floor (50%) or lighten the model in the render, not the tint rule. **Subtitle (GAP-072, proposed: needs your confirmation).** The line under the name is "klant / project". When the project equals the print name (case and spaces ignored) it shows the klant alone ("Joey" instead of "Joey / Babybox locks"); a different project still shows "Joey / Kerstversiering 2026". Same rule in PrintRow's sub line and in the breadcrumb of the print detail ("Joey" alone; the heading already names the print). The share page has no klant and shows the date instead.

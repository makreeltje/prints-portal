PrintCard is one print in the grid view: thumbnail, identity, key numbers, status and tags.

**Anatomy.** Thumbnail (4:3, server-rendered PNG on the neutral `surface-2` ground with a 4px filament bar along its bottom edge; see the tint rule under SpoolSwatch; or a "Geen voorbeeld" fallback), selection checkbox top-left as 24px of ink in a `--pp-tap` target (2.1), name (600), "klant / project" (`muted`), meta line (materiaal, print time with clock icon, grams), tags, then the price block on the left and the status badge on the right. Fluid width 220–320px; the grid uses `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(240px, 1fr))`, one column at 480px and below. **Deels betaald:** price block shows the remaining amount with the `pp-remaining` line, badge Deels betaald. **Niet factureren:** the price block shows "Kosten € x,xx" and the badge Niet factureren replaces the payment badge. **Verwijderd** (admin filter "Toon verwijderde"): thumbnail at 45% opacity, badge Verwijderd and a secondary small button "Herstellen" (one print; projects and customers have Herstellen in their header). **Gearchiveerd:** badge Gearchiveerd, thumbnail at 60% opacity. **Op factuur:** a locked print also shows the info badge "Op factuur {nummer}" in the tag row. **Mogelijk dubbel** appears only on the detail page.

**Tokens.** `surface`, `border`, `primary-tint-soft`/`primary` (selected), `primary-tint-strong` (live flash), `shadow-sm` (hover), `radius-lg`, `space-3`.

**States.** Default, hover, focus-visible (card link), selected, live update (1.6s flash, no layout shift), missing data, partial, niet factureren, archived, deleted.

**Accessibility.** The card is a link to the detail page with the name as accessible text; the checkbox and Herstellen are separate controls. Status is text plus icon.

**Orders (1.4).** A print with more than one order shows the **sum** of the order prices, the **least settled** status of its orders (Open before Deels betaald before Betaald; Afgeschreven counts as settled), the open amount over all orders ("Nog € x open") and "{n} bestellingen" in the meta line. A deleted Nabestelling no longer counts.

**1.9.** (2026-10-07) *(the tint floor below is superseded by 2.0; it is kept because the measurements that killed it are measurements of this rule)* **Tint floor (GAP-071, proposed; needs your measurement).** The thumbnail's ground is the filament colour, which hides a model on a dark spool. Rule: the app computes the relative luminance **L** of the filament colour (WCAG formula) and sets `--mix` on the thumbnail: **L ≥ 0.18: `--mix: 100%`** (colour as is); below that **`--mix = max(40%, L / 0.18 × 100%)`**. The ground is then `color-mix(in srgb, var(--c1) var(--mix), var(--border-strong))`: a dark spool is blended towards `border-strong`, a mid blue-grey whose luminance is about 0.17 to 0.19 in **both** themes, so the floor is the same in light and dark and no new token is needed. The existing 28% white sheen stays, and a 1px inset `border-strong` ring at 50% opacity (`::after`) keeps a black tile separate from the card. A black spool is a grey ground (40% black), a dark purple is a dusky purple: still recognisable, no longer swallowing the model. Light filaments (L ≥ 0.18) are unchanged. `--mix` is a new allowed inline data property next to `--c1`. **To measure (owner):** the rendered model colour of a thumbnail (a dark grey model) and the ground colour of a black and of a dark purple tile, in the app after this change; I need the contrast ratio between them, aiming at 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11 for a graphic). If a dark model on the 40% floor is below 3:1, raise the floor (50%) or lighten the model in the render, not the tint rule. **Subtitle (GAP-072, proposed: needs your confirmation; confirmed in 2.0).** The line under the name is "klant / project". When the project equals the print name (case and spaces ignored) it shows the klant alone ("Joey" instead of "Joey / Babybox locks"); a different project still shows "Joey / Kerstversiering 2026". Same rule in PrintRow's sub line and in the breadcrumb of the print detail ("Joey" alone; the heading already names the print). The share page has no klant and shows the date instead.

**2.0.** (2026-10-08) **The tint, decided (GAP-071, resolved — a direction, not another number).** Your table settles it: no `--mix` value clears 3:1 at both ends, and at 80% and 60% the result is worse than doing nothing. That is not a bad number, it is the wrong mechanism. **Contrast between an arbitrary user colour and any fixed ground cannot be guaranteed** — whatever ground we choose, some filament colour sits on top of it. Separation has to come from a shape cue, not from a colour pair, and the colour has to stop standing behind the thing it hides.

So: **the filament colour marks the object, never the ground behind it.**

- `.pp-thumb.tinted` is now the neutral `surface-2` ground in both themes — the same ground the share page took in 1.9 (GAP-079) for exactly this reason. The white sheen, the `color-mix()`, the inset ring and the data property **`--mix` are gone**; the app stops computing a luminance.
- The colour returns as a **4px bar along the bottom edge of the card thumbnail** (`.pp-print .pp-thumb.tinted::after`, from `--c1`, with a 1px `border-strong` hairline above it). It is a marker, never a ground: no render ever sits on it, so no 3:1 pair between colour and model exists to fail. The hairline gives the bar itself a 3:1 boundary on both grounds (`border-strong` on `surface-2`: 3.69 light, 3.32 dark).
- **The render carries its own outline.** Because the ground is now fixed, the one pair that matters is model against `surface-2`, and a black model in the dark theme would still vanish. The server-rendered PNG therefore outlines the model's silhouette with 1px of **`#6f7a96`** — the light `border-strong` value, baked in because an image cannot read tokens. Measured: 3.69:1 on light `surface-2`, 3.53:1 on dark `surface-2`, 4.29 and 3.99 on the two `surface` values. One ink works in both themes, which a theme-aware ground never could.
- In the list view the 40px thumbnail has **no** bar (`.pp-table .pp-thumb.sm.tinted::after{content:none}`): the row already carries the SpoolSwatch in its Materiaal cell, and 4px of 40 is a quarter of the picture. A model-strip button has no bar either.

**Breaking:** `--mix` leaves the contract; `.pp-thumb.tinted` and `.pp-plate.tinted` paint something else. A dark spool no longer tints anything, which is a visible change on every card.

**Which files a print lacks (GAP-090, resolved).** Your distribution decides the form: 70% of tiles carry nothing, 24% one type, 6% two or three, and the types are three. **Corrected figures (2.0.1, your re-measurement):** no G-code **22%** (18 of 83), no 3MF **8%** (7), no STL **8%** (7) — the 16/5/4% first published were the "only this type is missing" counts under the wrong heading. The corrected per-type numbers reconcile with the distribution (24×1 + 4×2 + 2×3 = 38 = 22+8+8), which the first set did not; the shape of the answer is unchanged.

- The indicator is **one chip, not three icons**: `span.pp-lacks` — a `triangle-alert` icon and the word **"Ontbreekt:"** followed by the missing types in the fixed order **G-code, 3MF, STL** — ordered by consequence, not frequency (no G-code means it was never sliced here; 3MF and STL are both 8%, so frequency cannot order them) ("Ontbreekt: G-code", "Ontbreekt: G-code, 3MF, STL"). `warning` on `warning-tint` over an opaque `surface` base, exactly as a badge since 1.8, so it measures the same on a hovered or selected card.
- **Why not bare icons** (that half of the idea is rejected): three new glyphs with a slash would have to be invented, read as a legend you have to learn, and still need a text alternative; at 12px the word is shorter than the legend. At the long end the chip is 27 characters, which fits one line in a 240px card.
- It takes **no space when nothing is missing** — the element is absent, not empty, so 70% of the grid is unchanged. On the card it is the first item of the tag row; in PrintRow it goes in the Status cell under the badge; on the print detail it sits in the header badge row and is repeated as a sentence in "Deze print".
- Accessible name: "Deze print heeft geen G-code-bestand." (one per missing type, joined). Never colour alone: the word and the icon carry it.
- **Settled by measurement and left out:** video (it would mark 40% of tiles and a timelapse is not expected of every print) and "incomplete 3MF" (the proxy matched 92%, so it marked almost everything).

**One target per tile (GAP-091, resolved; GAP-093 and GAP-099 merge into it).** The tile opens the print; the checkbox selects. The rule and the reason are in README under *Targets*. On the card: the stretched link keeps the whole tile as the one target (GAP-070, unchanged); the checkbox chip is now visibly its own control (a `border-strong` hairline on the `surface` chip and the full `--pp-tap` hit area) and sits above the link; the tile never selects on a click, because a click that does two things is worse than one that is learned. `is-selected` (the `primary` border and tint) appears only after the checkbox is ticked, so nothing looks selected before anything is.

**Subtitle (GAP-072, resolved — yes).** The 1.9 proposal is confirmed as it stands and is official: the second line is "{klant} / {project}", and the klant alone when the project equals the print name (case, spaces and punctuation ignored); an empty project is the same case. PrintRow's sub line, the print-detail breadcrumb and the share-page line follow it; the share page shows the date instead of the klant it has no name for. 1.9 marked this *proposed* and the share page built it while `placeOf` did not — both halves are now official, with no other change.

**2.1.** (2026-10-08) **Icons, because that is what was asked for twice (GAP-106, resolved; my 2.0 answer overruled by the owner).** I rejected the icon form and specified the word chip, you built it as specified, and on first sight the owner said they had expected simple icons, not text. That is the second time they have asked. It is their product, they have now seen the result rather than imagined it, and the chip measured 27 characters on a 261px card — the widest thing in the tag row. The decision stands with them.

My three objections were real, so the icon form answers each of them rather than ignoring them:

- **"Three inventions."** Nothing is invented. The three icons are the ones the system already names: `file-code` (G-code), `package` (3MF), `box` (STL), at **16px** in `warning`. "Missing" is a **diagonal slash** drawn corner to corner in the same SVG (`<line class="sl">` at 1.75px, from bottom-left to top-right) — the ordinary "no" convention, not a new glyph vocabulary. A second line underneath (`class="sl-bg"`, 3px in `var(--surface)`) knocks a gap out of the glyph so the slash still reads at 16px; it is drawn first, the `warning` line over it.
- **"A legend to learn."** There is now a place where the legend is written: **icons on the card and the row, the worded chip on the print detail** (`pp-lacks has-text`, "Ontbreekt: G-code, 3MF, STL"), plus the full sentence in the "Deze print" card. Short where space is short, worded where there is room — which was one of your three suggested directions and the only one that teaches.
- **"It still needs a text alternative."** It has one. The group is `role="img"` with `aria-label` and `title` carrying the whole sentence: **"Deze print heeft geen G-code-bestand."**, and for several, items joined in Dutch — "Deze print heeft geen G-code-bestand, geen 3MF-bestand en geen STL-bestand." Never colour alone: the slash carries it, and the sentence carries it for anyone who cannot see either.

**No pill.** The `warning-tint` background goes with the words; 1–3 icons in a tinted pill is a blob. The icons sit on the card ground directly, and they still clear 3:1 everywhere they land: `warning` on `surface` **6.11** light / **10.84** dark, on `surface-2` 5.26 / 9.60, on a hovered card (`primary-tint-soft` over `surface`) 5.46 / 9.53, on a selected one (`primary-tint`) **4.99** / 8.52. The worst case is 4.99 against a 3:1 requirement.

**Order and placement unchanged:** G-code, 3MF, STL by consequence; first item of the tag row; absent (not empty) when nothing is missing, which is **58%** of tiles on your rebuilt data.

**The selection box is 24px of ink in a 36px target (GAP-107, resolved).** You are right and you quoted the right rule back at me: WCAG 2.2 2.5.8 is about the **target**, not the paint, and 2.0 grew both — 36×36 of chip around a 20px checkbox, drawn 24 times a page whether anything is selected or not. The intent (it must read as its own control) was right; the execution was loud.

- `.pp-print .pp-sel` is now the **target only**: `--pp-tap` square (36px, 44px at 480px and below), no ground, no radius, no shadow, the label filling it so the whole square is clickable.
- The **ink is the checkbox plus a ring**: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px var(--surface), 0 0 0 3px var(--border-strong)` — a 20px box inside a 24px `surface` chip with a hairline at its edge. About a third less painted area than 2.0, and it still reads as a control on top of a picture, which is what the hairline was for.
- Focus moves out of the way of the hairline: `outline-offset: 4px` on the checkbox.
- Unchanged: the chip is always visible (hiding it until hover would lose it for keyboard users), it sits above the stretched card link, and a tile still never selects on a click.

**Where a price-less card puts its badge (GAP-108, resolved).** The ragged edge was not the new chip's fault; it was two things sharing a row that is built for two different things. `.pp-foot` is a `space-between` row with **exactly two slots**, and the rule is now explicit: **each slot holds the thing it is about.**

- **Left slot — the money.** The price, "Nog € x open" under it, "Kosten € x,xx" for a not-billed klant, or, when there is no customer price, the `warning` badge **"Geen prijs"**: it *is* the price, so it belongs in the price's place.
- **Right slot — the payment status.** Betaald / Open / Deels betaald / Niet factureren / Verwijderd / Gearchiveerd, or **nothing**. A print without a price has no payment status, so the slot is empty, and `space-between` leaves the single left item at the left edge. The ragged edge disappears without a class.
- **Neither slot is for missing data.** "Geen spoel" and the missing-file icons belong to the **tag row** above, with the other things that describe the print rather than its money. That is where 2.0 already put the icons; "Geen spoel" joins them.
- If a slot needs two elements (price plus remainder), they are wrapped in one `<span>`, as the partial-payment case already does. Two bare children of `.pp-foot` is what produced 141px of gap between two identical-looking chips.

On the 30% of this library with no price, the bottom of the card is now: tag row (tags, missing files, missing spool) left-aligned, then one "Geen prijs" badge at the left. **No CSS change** — this is a markup rule, and the preview draws it.

**Dutch copy.** No new keys. `print.lacks` ("Ontbreekt: {types}") is now the print-detail form only; `print.lacks.aria` becomes the joined sentence, with one new item key `print.lacks.item` ("geen {type}-bestand").

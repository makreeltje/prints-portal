EmptyState explains why a list is empty and offers the next step.

**Anatomy.** Dashed `border-strong` panel, 56px illustration slot (icon by default, a small diagram on the first-run screen), title, one sentence, one action. Variants: geen prints, geen resultaten, prints-map onbereikbaar.

**Tokens.** `surface`, `border-strong`, `surface-2`, `muted`, `text-strong`, `radius-lg`.

**States.** Static. The "geen resultaten" variant offers "Filters wissen"; the unreachable variant offers "Opnieuw proberen".

**Accessibility.** Title is a heading at the right level; the illustration is `aria-hidden`.

**Dutch copy.** Nog geen prints | Zet je bestanden in klant/project-mappen en start een index. [Opnieuw indexeren] | Geen resultaten | Pas je filters aan of wis ze. [Filters wissen] | Prints-map niet bereikbaar | Controleer of de map gekoppeld is.

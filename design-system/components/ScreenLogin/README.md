Login (`/login`) offers local sign-in with an e-mail address or a name plus password and, when configured, "Inloggen met Authentik" (OIDC). After a first sign-in with a temporary password the user must choose a password before anything else is reachable.

**Anatomy.** **Card:** `pp-auth` (centred, max 380px, `surface`, `border`, `radius-lg`, `space-4` padding and gap) on `bg`: studio name or logo, heading "Inloggen" (`heading-2xl`), FormFields "E-mailadres of naam" (`type="text"`, `autocomplete="username"`; the backend stores a lower-cased login name that may be an e-mail address or a name) and Wachtwoord (`autocomplete="current-password"`), primary button "Inloggen" (full width), the divider `.or` ("of") and the secondary button "Inloggen met Authentik". Only the methods that are configured are rendered: with OIDC only, the form and divider are replaced by the Authentik button; with local only, the divider and the Authentik button are omitted. **Errors:** wrong credentials show an `unpaid` alert under the heading and keep the typed name; a failed Authentik sign-in (the provider sends the browser back to `/login`) shows an `unpaid` alert "Inloggen met Authentik is mislukt. Probeer het opnieuw." above the buttons; HTTP 429 shows a `warning` alert with a clock icon and disables the submit button until the minute has passed; an ended session (60 minutes without activity or 12 hours after signing in) shows an `info` alert "Je sessie is verlopen. Log opnieuw in." **Nieuw wachtwoord kiezen** (shown after signing in with a temporary password, no sidebar or navigation): same card with heading "Nieuw wachtwoord kiezen", the sentence "Je gebruikt een tijdelijk wachtwoord. Kies een eigen wachtwoord om verder te gaan.", FormFields "Nieuw wachtwoord" (help "Minstens 12 tekens.") and "Herhaal wachtwoord" (both `autocomplete="new-password"`), primary "Opslaan" and ghost "Uitloggen". Errors are field errors: "Kies een wachtwoord van minstens 12 tekens." and "De wachtwoorden zijn niet gelijk." Authentik users never see this card. On phones the card is full width with `space-4` margins and 44px controls.

**Tokens.** `bg`, `surface`, `border`, `radius-lg`, `space-4`, `heading-2xl`, Button, FormField, Alert tokens. Classes: `pp-auth`, `pp-auth .or`.

**States.** Default, submitting (spinner), wrong credentials, too many attempts, Authentik failed (with and without the local form), session ended, Authentik redirecting ("Doorsturen naar Authentik…"), new password (default, too short, not equal, saving).

**Accessibility.** Autofocus the first field; errors use `role="alert"` (the session notice `role="status"`); field errors are linked with `aria-describedby`; Enter submits.

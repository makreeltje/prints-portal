PaymentPanel sets the Verkoopprijs and records payment, partial payment and write-off for one print.

**Anatomy.** Card titled "Betaling". **Open:** NumberInput "Verkoopprijs" (`€` prefix) with the "voorgesteld" hint until saved, the buttons "Voorstel uit marge (400%)" and Opslaan, then "Betaald bedrag" (defaults to the remaining amount) and the primary "Markeer als betaald". An amount below the remaining amount registers a partial payment. **Deels betaald:** badge, total price, "Betaald € 5,00 van € 11,89", the `pp-remaining` line "Nog € 6,89 open", a short payment history (date, amount), then the amount field and the actions Markeer als betaald and Afschrijven (the write-off covers the remainder). **Betaald:** badge, amount, "Betaald op {datum}", history; Admins also get the ghost button "Betaling ongedaan maken" (ConfirmDialog). **Afgeschreven:** badge, and a definition list with Betaald, Afgeschreven, Datum, Door and Reden; Admins get "Afschrijving ongedaan maken" (ConfirmDialog). A write-off after a partial payment keeps both amounts visible. **Niet factureren:** the whole panel is replaced by the info note "Deze klant wordt niet gefactureerd." and the cost ("Kosten"). **Rol User:** read-only badge and customer price, no inputs, no history. **Vergrendeld:** when the print is on a final invoice the badge "Op factuur {nummer}" shows, the price field is disabled with the help text, and payment stays possible. **Kosten ontbreken:** the suggestion button is disabled with a tooltip.

**Tokens.** `surface`, `border`, `heading-lg`, `paid`, `unpaid`, `info`, `warning`, `*-tint`, button and input tokens.

**States.** Open with suggestion, open with saved price, saving, partial, paid, written off, niet factureren, user (read-only), locked, missing cost, error (inline alert, values kept).

**Accessibility.** Each input has a label; the suggestion result and the remaining amount are announced with `aria-live="polite"`; undo and write-off always open a ConfirmDialog; admin-only actions are not rendered for other roles.

Toggle switches a setting on or off immediately, such as "Telt niet mee in kosten".

**Anatomy.** 40 x 24px track with a round thumb; label to the right or left; applies on change without a Save button.

**Tokens.** `surface-2`, `border-strong`, `primary`, `on-primary`, `text-strong` (thumb), `motion-fast`.

**States.** Off, on, hover, focus-visible, disabled, loading (disable it while the request runs and roll back with a toast on failure).

**Accessibility.** Use `<input type="checkbox" role="switch">`; the label states the setting, not the action.

**Dutch copy.** Telt niet mee in kosten, Live updates, Donker thema.

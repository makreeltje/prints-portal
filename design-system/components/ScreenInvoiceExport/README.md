The invoice export dialog creates a Concept download or a final invoice for one klant; the share page has a reduced version.

**Anatomy.** **Editors:** dialog "Factuur maken voor {klant}": two DatePickers (Van, Tot en met; the period filters on the **order date**, see OrderSection, and the dialog opens with every order of the klant that is not on a final invoice yet), the checkbox "Alleen openstaand", a preview card with Regels, Totaal, Betaald and Openstaand (no VAT anywhere), and a `warning` alert that names the prints left out because they have no customer price. A full invoice includes paid prints; with "Alleen openstaand" only unpaid and partially paid lines appear (partial lines show the remainder in the document). The dialog is the wider `pp-dialog lg` (560px) because three buttons do not fit 440px; the footer also wraps (`flex-wrap`) when the screen is narrower. Footer: Annuleren, secondary "Concept downloaden" (unnumbered PDF titled "Concept"), primary "Definitief maken" (refused until Bedrijfsgegevens has a name and an IBAN: the button is `aria-disabled` and a `warning` alert "Naam en IBAN ontbreken." with a link to Instellingen explains it; a Concept still downloads, without the payment sentence; opens the ConfirmDialog that names klant, number of prints and amount; creates the numbered invoice YYYY-NNN, stores PDF and snapshot and locks the lines: one invoice line per order, a "Voorstel uit marge" becomes the order's Verkoopprijs, and price, cost adjustments and "Aantal keer geprint" are locked ("Op factuur {nummer}"); payments and write-offs still work). **Share-page visitors:** only the checkbox "Alleen openstaand" and the button "Download overzicht" (PDF "Overzicht", customer price only). **Phone:** full-height sheet, buttons stacked and full width with the primary first. The dialog is not available for klanten that are niet factureren.

**Tokens.** Dialog, DatePicker, Checkbox, Alert tokens; `surface-2` for the preview card.

**States.** Default, no lines (primary buttons disabled with the help "Geen regels in deze periode. | Naam en IBAN ontbreken. | Vul ze in bij Instellingen, Bedrijfsgegevens. | Naar Instellingen"), all prints without price, generating (spinner), error.

**Accessibility.** `role="dialog"`; the preview card is `aria-live="polite"` and updates as the filters change; excluded prints are listed as text.

**Inline values (1.4).** The preview no longer uses `style` attributes for layout: widths, gaps, alignment and weights come from `pp-w-*`, `pp-gap-*`, `pp-between`, `pp-end`, `pp-nowrap`, `pp-setrow`, `pp-kv tight`, `pp-bg-2`, `pp-sheetframe` and the other helpers listed in `changelog.md`. Spool and print colours remain the data properties `--c1` and `--c2`.

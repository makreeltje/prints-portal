The invoice export dialog creates a Concept download or a final invoice for one klant; the share page has a reduced version.

**Anatomy.** **Editors:** dialog "Factuur maken voor {klant}": two DatePickers (Van, Tot en met; the period filters on the **order date**, see OrderSection, and the dialog opens with every order of the klant that is not on a final invoice yet), the checkbox "Alleen openstaand", a preview card with Regels, Totaal, Betaald and Openstaand (no VAT anywhere) plus, under the Regels row, the `muted` `.pp-reason` line naming the prints left out as **niet doorgegaan** (2.9), and a `warning` alert that names the prints left out because they have **no customer price**. Those are two different exclusions with two tones and they never share a line — see 2.9 below. A full invoice includes paid prints; with "Alleen openstaand" only unpaid and partially paid lines appear (partial lines show the remainder in the document). The dialog is the wider `pp-dialog lg` (560px) because three buttons do not fit 440px; the footer also wraps (`flex-wrap`) when the screen is narrower. Footer: Annuleren, secondary "Concept downloaden" (unnumbered PDF titled "Concept"), primary "Definitief maken" (refused until Bedrijfsgegevens has a name and an IBAN: the button is `aria-disabled` and a `warning` alert "Naam en IBAN ontbreken." with a link to Instellingen explains it; a Concept still downloads, without the payment sentence; opens the ConfirmDialog that names klant, number of prints and amount; creates the numbered invoice YYYY-NNN, stores PDF and snapshot and locks the lines: one invoice line per order, a "Voorstel uit marge" becomes the order's Verkoopprijs, and price, cost adjustments and "Aantal keer geprint" are locked ("Op factuur {nummer}"); payments and write-offs still work). **Share-page visitors:** only the checkbox "Alleen openstaand" and the button "Download overzicht" (PDF "Overzicht", customer price only). **Phone:** full-height sheet, buttons stacked and full width with the primary first. The dialog is not available for klanten that are niet factureren.

**Tokens.** Dialog, DatePicker, Checkbox, Alert tokens; `surface-2` for the preview card.

**States.** Default, no lines (primary buttons disabled with the help "Geen regels in deze periode. | Naam en IBAN ontbreken. | Vul ze in bij Instellingen, Bedrijfsgegevens. | Naar Instellingen"), all prints without price, generating (spinner), error.

**Accessibility.** `role="dialog"`; the preview card is `aria-live="polite"` and updates as the filters change, which is what announces the declined line when a filter moves one in or out; excluded prints are listed as text in both forms.

**2.9.** (2026-10-17) **What a document leaves out, it names (GAP-139, resolved — adopted from the implementation).** 2.8 said a declined quote is "excluded from every total", which is satisfied by **silence** — and silence is the one outcome the operator cannot check. The app names the declined order in the draft's exclusions instead, which is better than the spec, and it becomes the rule: **an amount left out of a document is named in that document, with its reason.**

**This was already the habit here and I failed to state it as one.** This dialog has carried a `warning` alert naming the prints left out for having no customer price since 1.0 (`{n} prints zonder verkoopprijs zijn weggelaten: {namen}.`), the preview card is `aria-live="polite"`, and the README already says excluded prints are listed as text. The rule generalises that: every exclusion from a Concept, a final invoice or an Overzicht is visible in the thing it was excluded from, never only in the total it is missing from.

**2.9, amended — the exclusion list splits, because one block cannot carry two tones (GAP-140, resolved; GAP-141, resolved; GAP-142, resolved).** The build put declined orders into the same `excluded` array as price-less ones, and the dialog rendered that array through `invoice.omitted` in a `pp-alert warning` with a `triangle-alert`. So a print quoted at € 24,50 and refused was announced as **having no sale price, in orange**. Both halves are false: the reason is wrong, and `warning` says something needs doing on the one line whose point is that nothing does. It is the GAP-120 fault and the GAP-126 fault in one sentence, introduced by the change that was meant to remove exactly this class of error. **The naming was right; it was put in the wrong sentence and the wrong ink.**

**1. Two conditions, two lines, two tones.** They are different kinds of fact and the dialog must say so.

| left out because | ink and form | why |
|---|---|---|
| **no sale price** — an input is missing | the existing `pp-alert warning` with `triangle-alert`, **unchanged** | There is something to do: set a price and make the invoice again. `warning` is correct and stays. |
| **niet doorgegaan** — a decision was taken | a `muted` `.pp-reason` line **in the preview card**, no alert, no border, **no glyph** | Nothing is to be done. This is the ruling of GAP-120 applied to the same shape of problem: *a warning is for something that needs doing; a fact that needs nothing gets plain ink.* `info` is not right either — `info` is for notices and hints (`voorgesteld`, Spoolman) that explain or suggest; this is a statement of what the document contains. |

**2. Where the declined line goes, and why not a second alert.** Inside the preview card, directly **under the `Regels` row**, because it qualifies that count — the operator is reading "Regels: 7" and this says what is not among the seven.

- The preview card is already `aria-live="polite"` and updates as the filters change (1.0). A declined print entering or leaving the chosen period is precisely a filter-driven change, so the line is announced at the right moment **for free**. A second alert would either need its own live handling or be announced *as an alert*, which overstates a fact that needs nothing.
- A second bordered block is real cost in this dialog: 560px already holding two DatePickers, a checkbox, the preview card, one `warning` alert and three footer buttons, with a footer that wraps. The exclusion that needs attention keeps the only alert; the one that does not needs no box at all.
- **No glyph**, deliberately. `.pp-reason` carries an info glyph in AssetListItem because it must be picked out of a dense list of file rows; here it is the only prose among figures and nothing competes with it, so a glyph would be the single icon in that card. It also sidesteps `.pp-reason`'s `align-items:center`, which would float a glyph in the vertical middle of a sentence that wraps to two or three lines.
- **No new class and no CSS change:** `.pp-reason` is `text-xs`/`leading-xs` in `muted` and has meant exactly this since 2.7. `muted` on `surface-2` (the preview card's ground) is in `contrast.md` at 4.5:1 or better in **both themes**; no new pair.

**3. The copy.** Both keys take ICU plurals, which the existing one lacked — "1 prints zonder verkoopprijs **zijn** weggelaten" is live today and n = 1 is the common case for that alert (GAP-141).

- **`invoice.omitted`, changed:** `{count, plural, one {# print zonder verkoopprijs is weggelaten} other {# prints zonder verkoopprijs zijn weggelaten}}: {names}.`
- **`invoice.omittedDeclined`, new:** `{count, plural, one {# print is niet doorgegaan en wordt niet gefactureerd} other {# prints zijn niet doorgegaan en worden niet gefactureerd}}: {names}.`

The declined line borrows the badge's own words — *niet doorgegaan* — on purpose: the operator has seen that badge on the print, and the line and the badge must speak one language. "wordt niet gefactureerd" states the consequence in the present tense, which is true here and is the thing they actually need to know.

**4. The long case, which both lines have and neither handled (GAP-141).** `{names}` is unbounded. Eighty-three prints without a price would list eighty-three names in one sentence in a 560px dialog, and after a migration pass the declined list will not be short either. **Both lines name at most five, then `invoice.omittedMore`: `en {count} andere`.** `do-dont.md` has required a cap on any list that grows since 2.2; this alert has been exempt by oversight. Five fits two to three lines at 560px and still answers the question the line exists to answer, which is *whether the one I am thinking of is in there*.

**5. It is the operator's surface only, never the customer's copy — and this narrows a rule of mine (GAP-142).** 2.9 wrote "an amount left out of a document is named in that document", and read literally that puts the operator's internal exclusions on a **customer invoice**. That is wrong and it is my wording, not the build's. The rule is narrower: **the surface where the operator creates or reviews a document names what it leaves out.** Neither line appears in the Concept PDF, the final invoice, the Overzicht or the share page: a customer does not need a list of what they declined, and `do-dont.md` has forbidden internal reasoning on customer-facing output since 1.0.

**6. What this implies for the array, which is yours to decide.** Two sentences and two tones mean the dialog must be able to tell the two apart before it renders either. Whether that is two arrays or one array with a reason per entry is your call and both work; what the design needs is only that **the reason arrives with the entry**, so no screen has to infer from "has a price" why something was left out. If it stays one array, the dialog groups by reason and renders the `warning` group in the alert and the declined group in the card — the grouping is then in the dialog, which is fine, because the grouping is a presentation decision and that is where presentation decisions live.

StatusBadge states payment, record, billing, asset or document status at a glance; it always carries an icon and a word.

**Anatomy.** Pill-ish tag (`radius-sm`), 16px icon, 12px label in 500. **Payment:** Betaald (`paid`, circle-check), Open (`unpaid`, clock), Deels betaald (`unpaid`, circle-dashed; the print stays open for the remainder), Afgeschreven (`unpaid`, ban). **Record:** Gearchiveerd (neutral, archive), Verwijderd (neutral, trash-2). **Completeness:** Geen spoel, Geen prijs, Kosten ontbreken (`warning`, triangle-alert). **Billing:** Niet factureren (neutral, receipt; replaces the payment badge for customers flagged niet factureren). **Asset:** Mogelijk dubbel (`warning`, copy). **Document:** Op factuur {nummer} (`info`, file-text, on locked prints), Concept (neutral, file-text), Definitief (`info`, lock), Creditnota (neutral, file-text). Show at most one payment or billing badge plus the completeness badges that apply. A partially paid print also shows `status.remaining` ("Nog € 6,89 open") as a separate `pp-remaining` line, never inside the badge.

**Tokens.** `paid`/`unpaid`/`warning`/`info` on their `*-tint` grounds, `surface-2`, `muted`, `border`.

**States.** Static. In dense tables the label may collapse to the icon at 480px and below, keeping `aria-label` and a tooltip.

**Accessibility.** Text and icon colour pass 4.5:1 on the tint (see contrast.md, including the neutral badge). Never rely on the colour; the word is always in the DOM. Deels betaald and Open share a colour on purpose; the dashed icon and the word tell them apart.

**Dutch copy.** Betaald, Open, Deels betaald, Afgeschreven, Gearchiveerd, Verwijderd, Geen spoel, Geen prijs, Kosten ontbreken, Niet factureren, Mogelijk dubbel, Op factuur {nummer}, Concept, Definitief, Creditnota, Nog {bedrag} open.

**1.8.** (2026-10-07) **Opaque base (GAP-061, resolved).** The tinted badges (`paid`, `unpaid`, `warning`, `info`) and the `pp-hint` chip are painted as the tint over an opaque `surface` base: `background: linear-gradient(var(--x-tint), var(--x-tint)), var(--surface)`. Their ground is therefore always `surface`, whatever sits behind them: a hovered row (`primary-tint-soft`), a selected row (`primary-tint`), `surface-2` or `bg`. Without the base, a light `unpaid` badge on a selected row measures 4.10 and on a hovered row 4.44 (contrast report, *Translucent token inside a translucent row*); with it the ratio is the one of the first table (4.93 `unpaid`, 4.98 `warning`, 5.33 `paid`, 5.23 `info` over `surface`). A badge on a coloured row therefore shows as a slightly lighter pill; there is no token change. The `neutral` badge was already opaque (`surface-2` with a border). Rule: a new translucent token that can sit inside a tinted row gets the same opaque base, or is listed in the nested table of `contrast.md` and passes.

**2.8.** (2026-10-17) **The payment group means money owed, and may not carry a request lifecycle (GAP-126, rejected).** The app's order status is `open | partial | paid | writtenOff | noPrice | notBilled` and every value of it concerns money already owed; this badge is the thing the whole UI reads to learn that. *Aangevraagd* and a declined quote are not money owed — one is money not yet asked for, the other money that was asked for and refused — so **neither may be rendered in the payment group**, and the completeness group is not theirs either (`Geen prijs`, `Geen spoel`, `Kosten ontbreken` all mean *an input is missing from a record*, where a declined quote is missing nothing). Whatever the app decides to store, the display rule is fixed: a request stage is **its own group** in this badge, neutral or `info`, never `paid`/`unpaid`/`warning`, and a print carries at most one badge from it alongside at most one payment or billing badge.

**The trap, which is the whole reason this is a ruling and not a preference.** `Afgeschreven` is `unpaid` + `ban` because that money *was* owed and will not arrive. A quote the customer turned down was **never owed**. If a declined quote shows in `unpaid`, every openstaand-shaped surface in the app — the badge, the klant chip, Openstaand, the invoice export — reads it as a debt, and the operator's own figures become wrong in the direction that matters. `unpaid` on a number nobody ever agreed to pay is the one reading this system does not allow.

**Proposed, needs your confirmation — how a declined quote reads.** The owner wants the price kept, so a page must show money for something that will never be paid.

- **Badge:** the `neutral` form (`surface-2` with a `border`, as `Gearchiveerd` has since 1.0), 16px `circle-x`, and the word **"Niet doorgegaan"**. Not `ban`, which is `Afgeschreven`'s. And deliberately **not** the word *Afgewezen*: it differs from *Afgeschreven* by three letters in the middle of a long word, the two would sit in the same column of the same list, and the system has just spent three rounds on the fact that a difference in the middle of a string is the hardest one to see. "Niet doorgegaan" is also what the owner actually said happened.
- **The amount:** `muted`, not `text-strong`, prefixed **"was"** — `was € 24,50`. **No strikethrough.** MoneyDisplay's `<s>` means *this price was replaced by that one*; this price was not replaced, it was refused, and striking it would read as a correction.
- **Every total:** excluded. Openstaand, the klant chip's openstaand figure, the dashboard totals, the invoice export and the share page do not see it. It is a record, not a receivable. Where a klant has only declined jobs the chip reads "Alles betaald" as it does today — that line is about debt and there is none.
- **The date:** an asserted fact, so it carries one in `muted` ("Niet doorgegaan op 3 oktober"), per rule 1 of `README.md`.
- **Both themes, no new colour.** `neutral` on `surface-2` and `muted` on `surface`/`surface-2` are both already in `contrast.md` at 4.5:1 or better in both themes. **No new token and no new colour pair** — which is the point: the distinction is carried by a word, an icon and ink weight, and nothing here needs a hue.

**The Dutch wording above is a proposal and is deliberately not in `copy-deck.md`.** The deck is the register of live copy; putting a key there would read as an instruction to build, and the state it names does not exist yet. Confirm the direction and the keys go in with the version that builds it. Icons for *printed* and *delivered* are not proposed here, except to rule two out: not `circle-check` (that is `Betaald`) and not `package` (that is 3MF).

**2.8, amended — it is three states, not one (GAP-127, widened; built in 2.9).** The entry above specified the badge for a refused quote and called that the gap. It is one third of it. Asked to set a state on every print in his library, the owner named **six** states unprompted: paid, unpaid, ordered again, asked-and-refused, asked-and-undecided, asked-and-in-progress. The first three exist (`paid`, `open`, and a second order). **Three have no home**, and they are the request group:

| state | word (proposed) | icon (proposed) | ink |
|---|---|---|---|
| asked, nothing decided yet | **Nog niet besloten** | `circle-help` | `info` on `info-tint` |
| asked, being worked on | **Mee bezig** | `hammer` | `info` on `info-tint` |
| asked and refused | **Niet doorgegaan** | `circle-x` | neutral (`surface-2` + `border`) |

**The split of ink is the part worth keeping even if the words change: a live state takes the live ink and a settled negative one does not.** `Niet doorgegaan` is finished and needs nothing, so it is neutral, exactly as `Gearchiveerd` is; the two open states are notices, which is what `info` has always been for — and it keeps `warning` out of the group entirely, which is the point of GAP-126. All three pairs are already in `contrast.md` at 4.5:1 or better in **both themes**: `info` on `info-tint` over `surface` (5.23) and the neutral badge on `surface-2`. **No new token, no new colour pair, no new class.**

Least sure, and the owner's vocabulary should win: **"Nog niet besloten"** is three words where this column is usually one; **"Mee bezig"** is his phrasing where *"In bewerking"* is the standard Dutch term; and `hammer` is the weakest of the three icons. None of the three is in `copy-deck.md` and none goes in until the direction is confirmed.

**2.9.** (2026-10-17) **Built as specified, and one decision of the implementer's becomes the rule (GAP-127, confirmed as built; GAP-138, resolved).** `Niet doorgegaan` with the neutral badge, the amount `muted` and prefixed "was" with **no strikethrough**, the date, and absent from every total — verified in the app: a declined order leaves Openstaand (EUR 299,82 → 299,65 → 299,82 and back) and keeps its price. All three request words are in the app exactly as written above. **Nothing in this component changes.**

**"Geen status" is a fourth value, not a clear action — the owner's decision, and his reason is the one to keep.** The entry above named three states and said nothing about how one is removed, which invites a "Status wissen" button. The dialog has **four radios, one of them "Geen status"**, because **a state must always be able to go back: a klant can change their mind about one print.** That is a fact about the work, and it is why the fourth option is necessary rather than merely tidy — I had reached the same answer from UI symmetry, which is the weaker argument. *(I first credited this to the implementer; it was the owner's call and the correction belongs here.)* Underneath it: clearing and setting become the same act with the same result shape, and nothing destructive-looking is offered for an act that destroys nothing. **The system had already decided it twice without my noticing** — "Geen spoel" is the last option of the spool list (BulkDialogs, 1.4) and "Geen kleur" sits in the tag palette (ScreenTags). It is a pattern now: see BulkDialogs and `do-dont.md`.
